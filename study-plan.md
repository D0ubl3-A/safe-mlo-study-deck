# SAFE MLO Study Plan for Two

Use this as a low-pressure way to help her study and spend time together. Keep each session short, specific, and useful.

## Send Her This

I made you a cleaner study deck from the SAFE MLO materials so we can drill together without it feeling overwhelming. We can do 25 minutes at a time: I quiz you, you explain the answer out loud, and we save the missed ones for the next round.

## What Is Included

- 728 total study items
- 399 direct flashcards
- 79 quick-rule cards
- 59 wrong-answer trap cards
- 188 multiple-choice practice questions
- 3 missed-question study steps

## Best Study Flow

1. Warm up with 15 mixed flashcards.
2. Drill one category she is weakest on for 15 minutes.
3. Run 10 quiz questions with no hints.
4. Re-study every missed card immediately.
5. End with 5 trap cards so exam wording gets easier to spot.

## Seven-Session Plan

| Session | Focus | Goal |
|---:|---|---|

## Rule for Studying Together

If she gets it right, ask: "Why is that the answer?" If she misses it, do not lecture. Read the correct rule, ask her to say it back in her own words, then mark it missed and move on.
